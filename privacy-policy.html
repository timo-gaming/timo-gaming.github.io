Mistwater privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Mistwater is an idle fishing game made by one independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. In short: the game doesn't ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and your progress is stored on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (coins, fish, villages, settings and the name you choose for yourself in the game) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. If you make a backup in the game, the backup is a line of text that you copy and keep yourself.

What the game sends, and why

  • Weather. To show real weather at the fishing spots, the game asks the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) for the forecast at the map location of the fishing spot you're looking at. These are fixed coordinates of places in the game, not your own location. Like any website, MET Norway receives your device's IP address with the request. See MET Norway's privacy statement.
  • Error reports. If the game runs into a technical error, it sends a short report so the bug can be fixed: the error message, the game version, your phone model and Android version, screen size, how far you are in the game (country, number of catches) and your game settings. Reports don't include your name, contacts or location. They are collected through a Google Form, so Google processes them under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Bug reports you send. If you use "Report a bug" in the menu, the text you write is sent together with the same technical details as an error report, also through a Google Form.

Advertising

The game offers optional ads that you can choose to watch for rewards. When ads are shown, they are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game's menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases in the game are handled by Google Play. We don't receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation of which item was bought, so the game can give it to you. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google provides your Play Games profile name to show you on leaderboards, and your scores and a copy of your game progress are stored with Google Play Games so you can continue on another phone. Signing in is optional. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example, when your boats are full). They are created on the device; nothing is sent to us.

Children

Mistwater is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Error and bug reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the problems they describe. To ask about or request deletion of a report you sent, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
